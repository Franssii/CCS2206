LOYALTY

The political support of your population for the administration, measured in %. Determines immigration, productivity and social stability.

INCOME (green) - loyalty rises when:
  - Citizens have stable employment
  - Public services are well funded
  - The economy is growing
  - Healthcare keeps the sick count low

EXPENSES (red) - loyalty falls when:
  - Unemployment is high
  - Many citizens are sick
  - The treasury runs into heavy debt
  - Power blackouts disrupt daily life

Tip: keep loyalty above 70% to attract immigrants and avoid political crisis. Below 40% emigration begins and unrest may break out.
