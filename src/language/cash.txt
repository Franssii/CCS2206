CASH (Treasury)

The total amount of money currently held by your city's treasury, expressed in dollars ($). Cash is your main resource for development - every road, building and factory has to be paid for from here.

INCOME (green) comes from:
  - Taxes paid by the employed citizens
  - Industrial production and exports
  - Loans granted by the central bank

EXPENSES (red) come from:
  - Constructing buildings, roads and factories
  - Maintenance costs of existing infrastructure
  - Unemployment and social benefits
  - Monthly loan repayments

Tip: keep cash above 10,000$ to ensure stable operations and avoid bankruptcy. If the treasury runs out, construction is blocked until new revenue arrives.
