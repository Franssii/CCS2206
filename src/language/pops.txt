POPULATION (Pops)

The total number of citizens currently living in your city. Population is the source of taxes, workforce and political support.

INCOME (green) - new citizens come from:
  - Births among existing population
  - Immigration when loyalty is high (>70%)
  - Workers attracted by job opportunities

EXPENSES (red) - citizens are lost to:
  - Natural deaths (especially among the sick)
  - Emigration when loyalty is low or jobs are scarce

Tip: population growth requires housing - build more residential blocks to accommodate newcomers, otherwise immigration stalls.
