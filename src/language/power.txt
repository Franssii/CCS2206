POWER (Energy)

The net electrical power balance of your city, expressed in MW. This is production minus consumption.

INCOME (green) - production:
  - Power plants (coal, oil, etc.)
  - Connected industrial facilities producing surplus

EXPENSES (red) - consumption:
  - Residential housing demand
  - Industrial machinery
  - Public infrastructure (street lighting, etc.)

Tip: keep the power balance above 50 MW for safety. A negative value causes blackouts which halt production, lower loyalty and slow population growth.
