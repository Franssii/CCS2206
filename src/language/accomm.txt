ACCOMMODATION (Accomm)

The number of citizens currently housed in residential buildings. Everyone else is homeless and vulnerable to emigration, sickness and loyalty loss.

GREEN line - newly housed:
  - Citizens moving into newly built homes
  - Population growth covered by housing expansion
  - Immigration when enough beds are available

RED line - homeless:
  - Citizens without a roof when population exceeds housing capacity
  - Homeless people may emigrate or fall ill more easily
  - Build more residential blocks to reduce this number

Tip: keep accommodation close to population. A large homeless population drains loyalty and slows city growth.
