WORKERS

The number of citizens currently employed in industrial buildings, factories and city services. Workers drive industrial output and tax revenue.

INCOME (green) - new workers come from:
  - Newly built factories and workplaces
  - Population growth providing more workforce
  - Returning emigrants when loyalty improves

EXPENSES (red) - workers are lost to:
  - Demolished or shut-down factories
  - Sickness and old age
  - Emigration

Tip: balance the worker count with available jobs. Too many idle workers means unemployment rises; too few workers cripples industry.
