SICK (Health)

The number of citizens currently incapacitated by illness. A lower value is always better - sick citizens cannot work, lower loyalty and may die.

GREEN line - recoveries:
  - Citizens healing naturally
  - Hospitals treating patients
  - Pollution reduction improving public health

RED line - new cases:
  - Pollution from heavy industry
  - Lack of healthcare infrastructure
  - Overcrowded residential areas

Tip: a high sick count cripples the workforce, lowers loyalty and increases deaths. Build hospitals and limit polluting industries to keep this number low.
